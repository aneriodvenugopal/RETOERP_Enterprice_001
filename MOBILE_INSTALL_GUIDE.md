## 📲 How to Install on Your Mobile (Step-by-Step)

### **For Android Phone:**

**Step 1: Open Chrome Browser**
- Open Google Chrome on your Android phone

**Step 2: Visit App URL**
```
https://tutorai-video-gen.preview.emergentagent.com/pwa/login
```

**Step 3: Login**
- Enter phone number: **9908290239** or **8888888888**
- Click "Send OTP"
- **OTP will appear in GREEN BOX on screen** 📱
- Copy the 6-digit OTP and enter it
- Click "Verify & Login"

**Step 4: Add to Home Screen**
- In Chrome, tap the **3 dots menu** (top right)
- Tap **"Add to Home Screen"**
- Edit name if needed (default: "RETOERP")
- Tap **"Add"**
- ✅ **Icon appears on home screen!**

**Step 5: Open App**
- Tap the RETOERP icon from home screen
- App opens full-screen (no browser bar)
- Looks exactly like native app!

## 💡 Common Issues & Solutions:

### **Issue 1: OTP Not Showing**
**Solution:**
- Make sure you're on latest page (refresh)
- OTP appears in GREEN BOX below "Verify OTP"
- Also check green toast at top
- Wait 2-3 seconds after clicking "Send OTP"

### **Issue 2: Icon Not Appearing**
**Solution:**
- After "Add to Home Screen", check home screen
- Icon might be on second page
- Look for blue "R" logo
- Try removing and re-adding

### **Issue 3: App Opens in Browser**
**Solution:**
- Don't use browser back button to open
- Tap the actual icon from home screen
- If still in browser, remove and re-add to home screen

### **Issue 4: Features Not Loading**
**Solution:**
- Check internet connection
- Token might have expired - logout and login again
- Clear app cache: Settings → Apps → RETOERP → Clear Cache
