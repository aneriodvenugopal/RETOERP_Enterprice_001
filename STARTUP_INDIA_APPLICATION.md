## 📋 **Executive Summary**

RETOERP is a comprehensive SaaS-based ERP platform specifically designed for the Indian real estate industry. We automate and streamline operations for real estate developers, reducing manual work by 40X through intelligent automation, AI-powered tools, and integrated workflows.

**Industry:** PropTech (Property Technology) / Real Estate SaaS  
**Target Market:** Real Estate Developers, Builders, Property Consultants in India  
**Stage:** MVP Complete, Early Revenue  
**Business Model:** B2B SaaS with subscription-based pricing

### **Challenges Faced by Indian Real Estate Companies:**

1. **Manual & Fragmented Operations** (90% of developers)
   - Excel sheets for lead tracking
   - WhatsApp for team communication

   - No centralized system

   - Payment delays and tracking issues

3. **Poor Customer Experience**
   - Customers have no visibility on payments
   - Manual payment reminders
   - Delayed documentation
   - No self-service portal

4. **Scalability Issues**
   - Cannot manage multiple projects efficiently
   - Staff productivity bottlenecks
   - No data-driven decision making
   - High operational costs

5. **Compliance & Trust**
   - RERA compliance challenges
   - Lack of transparency
   - Financial mismanagement
   - Customer trust issues

## 💡 **Our Solution - RETOERP**

### **What We Offer:**

A **complete, cloud-based ERP system** built specifically for Indian real estate companies that automates:

#### **1. Lead Management & CRM**
- AI-powered lead capture from multiple sources
- Automated follow-up workflows
- WhatsApp integration for instant communication
- Lead scoring and prioritization
- Conversion tracking

#### **2. Property Management**
- Interactive SVG-based plot layouts
- Visual property availability tracking
- Drag-and-drop plot editor
- Multi-project management
- RERA-compliant documentation

#### **3. Booking & Sales Automation**
- Digital booking process
- Multiple payment scheme support (12M, 18M, 24M, Custom)
- Automatic receipt generation
- Booking status tracking
- Customer portal access

#### **4. Payment Management**
- Razorpay integration (UPI, Cards, Net Banking)
- Manual entry (NEFT, Cheque, Cash)
- Payment reminders (SMS, Email, WhatsApp)
- Multi-currency support (INR, USD, EUR, GBP, AED, SGD)
- Real-time payment tracking

#### **5. Commission Management**
- Hierarchical staff structure
- Automatic commission calculation
- Gap commission for uplines
- Project-wise & category-wise commissions
- TDS calculation (5% auto-deducted)
- Agent payout system

#### **6. Project-Level Banking**
- Multi-role architecture (Tenant Admin, Project Admin)
- Project-specific bank accounts
- Role-based access control
- Financial isolation per project
- Automated reconciliation

#### **7. Multi-Tenant SaaS Platform**
- Unlimited tenants (real estate companies)
- Unlimited projects per tenant
- Usage tracking & limit enforcement
- Package-based pricing (Starter, Professional, Enterprise)
- Scalable infrastructure

#### **8. Customer Portal**
- Self-service dashboard for buyers
- View bookings and payments
- Download receipts
- Payment history
- Property resale requests

#### **9. AI-Powered Features**
- Content generation for marketing
- WhatsApp chatbot (24/7 support)
- Lead nurturing automation
- Sentiment analysis
- Predictive analytics

#### **10. IncomeLands Marketplace** (Unique Feature)
- B2B marketplace connecting developers with agents
- ₹10 contact unlock model
- Commission-based lead sharing
- Geo-location property search
- AI-powered buyer-property matching
