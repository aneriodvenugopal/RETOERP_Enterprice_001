RETOERP is a multi-tenant SaaS ERP platform designed for the Indian real estate market. The primary goal is to automate and streamline operations for real estate companies (tenants).

**Core Implemented Features:**
*   An interactive SVG plot layout editor.
*   An AI-powered content management system for generating marketing articles.
*   A multi-level property category management system (master, category, subcategory).
*   A customer-facing portal/dashboard for end-users to view their bookings, payments, and properties.
*   A tenant-level banking and credit system.

**Current Architectural Requirement:**
The user has mandated a critical change to the application's financial architecture. The current tenant-level banking system is insufficient. The new requirement is to implement a **project-specific banking system** governed by a strict role hierarchy:
1.  **Tenant Admin:** Has super-user access within their tenancy. They can create projects, create project admins, and view/manage bank accounts across **all** projects.
2.  **Project Admin:** A role created by the Tenant Admin and assigned to one or more specific projects. They can only create, view, and manage bank accounts associated with their **assigned project(s)**.

This change ensures financial data is properly isolated at the project level, reflecting real-world business operations.
</product_requirements>
