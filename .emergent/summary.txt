A significant portion of the work involved a complete overhaul of the Advisory feature. Simultaneously, the RETOERP Assistant (chatbot) was transformed from a simple Q&A bot into a multi-step lead generation and qualification funnel. It evolved to capture user requirements, verify contact information, and schedule appointments. This, too, was simplified at the user's request for a more direct sharp and simple lead capture flow.

The trajectory concludes with the AI engineer identifying a major gap: the entire frontend for the core Payment and Commission modules, despite having a complete backend, was never built. <product_requirements>
The objective is to build RETOERP, a multi-tenant SaaS ERP for real estate, with a focus on lead generation and core business process automation.

**Core ERP Modules:**
-   **Payment Receiving:** Support for custom payment schemes (installments, lump-sum), multi-currency, and both automated (Razorpay) and manual payment entries.
-   **Payment Transfer:** A hierarchical commission system that automatically calculates and tracks direct and gap commissions for a multi-level sales staff upon customer payment. Includes an approval workflow and payout tracking.

**Lead Generation & User Engagement:**
-   **Dual Advisory System:**
    -   **Public Users:** Receive a 100% free, template-based advisory enriched with real-time Google Places data to provide value and encourage sign-ups.
    -   **Tenant Users:** Access a premium, LLM-powered advisory service providing detailed, expert-level analysis.
-   **Intelligent Chatbot:** A simple and sharp assistant that provides basic information, captures user contact details, and allows them to schedule an appointment with an expert via a clean UI.
-   **Coupon System:** Generate unique discount coupons for users who engage with the advisory service to track lead conversion and incentivize property purchases.
-   **Workforce Data:** Scrape and display workforce data (e.g., plumbers, electricians) on an interactive map, with clickable statistics that open modals showing detailed contact information.

**UI/UX Requirements:**
-   The homepage must not have non-interactive elements that look like clickable buttons.
-   Forms should be user-friendly, with features like Google Maps autocomplete for location fields and flexible budget inputs.
</product_requirements>

<key_technical_concepts>
- **Frameworks**: FastAPI (Python) for backend, React.js for frontend.
- **Database**: MongoDB with Pydantic models.
- **APIs & Integrations**: Razorpay (payments), Google Places API (location data/autocomplete), Hugging Face (free AI alternative), Emergent LLM Key (for paid AI services).
- **Web Scraping**: BeautifulSoup4 for free data extraction.
- **Architecture**:
  - RESTful API design.
  - Dual-service model for the advisory feature (free vs. paid).
  - Rule-based chatbot for lead generation.
</key_technical_concepts>
